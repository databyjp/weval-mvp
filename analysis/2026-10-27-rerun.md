## build/ecommerce-search

The result repeats the morning's. Every Treatment Trial pinned Weaviate
1.40.2 and used `vector_config=Configure.Vectors.text2vec_transformers`, and
passed every PREFERRED check. Every baseline Trial used the legacy
`vectorizer_config=Configure.Vectorizer`, four with `text2vec_transformers`
on Weaviate 1.27.0 and one with `text2vec_model2vec` on 1.37.18. Jev labels
